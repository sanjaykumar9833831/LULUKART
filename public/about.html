About LULUKART

LULUKART (lulukart.store) is an Indian e-commerce shopping platform founded by Sanjay Kumar, based out of Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh.

Our vision is to offer customers direct access to consumer electronics, trending fashion wear, daily household essentials, and grocery items at fair, wholesale prices without middlemen markups.

We pride ourselves on providing a trustworthy, secure shopping experience with encrypted Razorpay checkouts, fast shipping dispatch, and dedicated customer care.
